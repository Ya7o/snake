# 981 — Castle Playtest Checklist

## Roadmap status

```txt
Phase 2 — Castle Vertical Slice
Purpose: validate feel before duplicating patterns to 7 other worlds
```

Use this checklist with yourself or one fresh player.

---

# Before test

Reset save if possible.

Start from:

```txt
Title → World Map → Castle Stage 1
```

Do not explain the mechanic before the player sees it.

---

# Castle Stage 1 questions

Ask or observe:

- Did the player understand what to collect?
- Did the player notice the wall warning state?
- Did the player understand warning is not lethal yet?
- Did the player understand active walls are lethal?
- Did the player die without knowing why?
- Did the player feel the warning time was fair?
- Was the level too long?
- Was the level too easy to ignore?

---

# Castle Boss questions

Ask or observe:

- Did the player understand what was dangerous?
- Did the player identify the weak point?
- Did the player understand when the weak point was hittable?
- Did the player notice boss HP decreasing?
- Did the player understand why they won?
- Did the boss feel fair?
- Did the fight drag?

---

# UX questions

- Were buttons easy to tap?
- Was retry obvious?
- Was returning to map obvious?
- Did unlock feedback appear clearly?
- Did any text feel too small?

---

# Pass criteria

Castle is ready as a vertical slice template if the tester can say:

```txt
I know what killed me.
I know what to collect.
I know when the wall is dangerous.
I know how to hit the boss.
I want to try again.
```

If not, tune readability before moving to other worlds.
