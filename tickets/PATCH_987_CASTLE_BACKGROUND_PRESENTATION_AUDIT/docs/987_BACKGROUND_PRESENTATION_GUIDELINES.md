# 987 — Background Presentation Guidelines

## Core principle

Each Castle background must be treated as a composition, not wallpaper.

## Screen type rules

### A. Intro / Stage card background
Protected art zones:
- top sky / moon zone;
- left and right tree / lantern columns;
- bottom path / stone area.

Runtime-safe zones:
- center-lower third for info panel;
- bottom action row for buttons.

Recommended visible art target:
```txt
At least 55–65% of the image remains directly visible.
```

### B. Gameplay background
Protected art zones:
- side framing columns;
- bottom architectural decoration;
- special focal elements.

Runtime-safe zones:
- central board region;
- small compact HUD anchors.

Recommended visible art target:
```txt
At least 25–35% of the image remains visible during gameplay.
```

### C. Game Over background
Recommended visible art target:
```txt
At least 60% of the image remains visible.
```

### D. Stage Clear / Boss Clear background
Recommended visible art target:
```txt
At least 58–68% of the image remains visible.
```

## Important challenge

A beautiful background plus a giant dark panel is not a premium UI.

It is a hidden background.
