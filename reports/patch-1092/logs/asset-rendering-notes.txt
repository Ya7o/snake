  → scene.textures.get(textureKey).setFilter(Phaser.Textures.FilterMode.LINEAR)

CSS on canvas element:

=== KNOWN PROBLEM: pickup_secondary.png ===
All 8 universes use a 32x32 pickup_secondary.png as the alternate pickup texture.
At the displayed size of ~57px CSS (114px physical at 2x DPR), this represents a
1.78x upscale. With LINEAR filter, this produces a blurry, pixelated appearance.
The secondary pickup in Sonic clearly shows this: the dark ring is fuzzy and unclear.

=== KNOWN PROBLEM: antialias:true ===
Phaser's antialias:true enables bilinear filtering on the WebGL context.
For pixel-art style sprites (obstacle PNGs like fighter fists, outrun cars),
this produces slight blurring that reduces the crisp retro aesthetic.
The effect is most visible on the 1254x1254 boss PNGs downscaled to ~50px.
Options: setFilter(NEAREST) per-texture for pixel art assets, or antialias:false globally.

=== CASTLE UNIVERSE ===
Uses exclusively SVG icons via CASTLE_OPENMOJI_ICON_ASSETS.
SVGs loaded at 64-128px, displayed at 3.0x cell (~69px CSS).
At DPR=2: 128px SVG → 138px physical → near 1:1 → sharp.
At DPR=1: 64px SVG → 69px physical → slight upscale → slightly soft.
Obstacle SVGs (brick, stone, door) rendered with very low alpha (~0.24-0.88).
On the dark maroon castle background, these become nearly invisible at low alpha states.
