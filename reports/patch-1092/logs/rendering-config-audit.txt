TEXTURE FILTERS
Sources: src/render/PickupRenderer.ts, src/render/ObstacleRenderer.ts, src/render/VfxUtils.ts

- PickupRenderer.fitImageInCell() sets FilterMode.LINEAR for every pickup texture.
- ObstacleRenderer.fitImageInCell() sets FilterMode.LINEAR for every obstacle and boss texture.
- VfxUtils includes an opt-in NEAREST helper, but the main runtime pickup/obstacle renderers do not use it for these assets.
- Consequence: 32x32 assets are blurred when upscaled, and 1254x1254 boss sprites become soft when downscaled to about 50px CSS.

LIKELY ROOT CAUSES

1. Low-resolution runtime icon assets (`pickup_secondary.png`).
2. Over-large generated PNGs downscaled with LINEAR.
3. No renderer-level distinction between pixel-art sprites and photographic/AI backgrounds.
4. Low contrast palettes in Kombat/Shinobi/Fighter/Streets boss states.
5. Procedural halos and glows too subtle for dark/red boards.
