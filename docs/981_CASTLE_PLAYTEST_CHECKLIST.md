# 981 Castle Playtest Checklist

Use after resetting save and starting `Castle Stage 1` from the world map.

## Stage 1

- Did the player know to collect the glowing objective?
- Did the player notice the faint wall before it became a warning?
- Did the player understand the warning wall was safe for a moment?
- Did the player understand the solid red wall was lethal?
- Did any death feel invisible or instant?
- Did the warning time feel fair on mobile portrait?

## Boss

- Did the player distinguish danger mirrors from the weak point?
- Did `FRAPPE` match the visible weak point timing?
- Did the hit flash/shake make boss damage obvious?
- Did the HP decrease feel clear?
- Was the boss beatable without verbal explanation?

## Clear Flow

- Was the Castle Boss unlock message visible after Stage 1?
- Was the World 1 complete message visible after the boss?
- Were Retry and Map still obvious after a death?
